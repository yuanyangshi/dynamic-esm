# CASF-2016 Gold-Standard Evaluation Protocol

This document outlines the benchmarking protocol for evaluating **Dynamic-ESM** on the **Comparative Assessment of Scoring Functions (CASF-2016)** benchmark and PDBbind.

---

## 1. Benchmark Powers Evaluated

CASF-2016 assesses scoring functions across four foundational computational drug discovery powers:

1. **Scoring Power**:
   - Pearson correlation coefficient ($R_p$) and RMSE between predicted and experimental binding affinities across 285 diverse protein-ligand complexes.
2. **Ranking Power**:
   - Spearman rank correlation ($\rho_s$) and Kendall's tau ($\tau$) measuring the ability to correctly rank known binders of the same target.
3. **Docking Power**:
   - Ability to identify native/near-native poses (RMSD $< 2.0\text{ \AA}$) among decoy conformations generated by docking tools.
4. **Screening Power**:
   - High-throughput virtual screening enrichment measured by Boltzmann-Enhanced Discrimination of ROC ($\text{BEDROC}$) and Enrichment Factors ($EF_{1\%}, EF_{5\%}$).

---

## 2. Statistical Validation Standards

All benchmark metrics in Dynamic-ESM are subjected to rigorous statistical verification:
- **1,000-resample Bootstrap 95% Confidence Intervals** ($[CI_{\text{lower}}, CI_{\text{upper}}]$).
- **Paired Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test** comparing absolute residuals of Dynamic-ESM against baselines (significance threshold: $p < 0.001$).
- **Bemis-Murcko Scaffold Hopping (OOD)**: Evaluation on test complexes with unseen molecular scaffolds to test zero-shot generalization.
